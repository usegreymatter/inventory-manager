# Inventory Manager v1.2.0 candidate

Unreleased candidate notes. Publication, hosted deployment and release downloads require separate owner action; these notes do not assert they have happened. Existing offline instructions remain in the [README](../../README.md).

## What changed

- **Fixed — profit shelf cap:** the opt-in suggestion and alternatives now receive the computed shelf ceiling. MOQ/pack conflicts omit the suggestion with an explicit standard-amount fallback; ordinary quantities are unchanged.
- **Fixed — preset cover floor:** each product covers only its own review interval via a product `months_of_cover` override when needed. Delivery time is not added twice, slow suppliers cannot inflate other products, and previews count the actual changed quantities.

- **Industry buttons are setup guides for ordering rhythm and shelf-life prompts, not quantity optimizers.** General, Grocery/food, Pharmacy, Fashion/seasonal, Spare parts and Electronics ask you to check the actual ordering interval and verified product shelf-life facts. They preserve `review_days`, never reduce existing cover and do not guess or write global shelf-life assumptions. **They do not change amounts on their own from General defaults.** Confirming an ordinary-setting change, such as raising insufficient cover or resetting custom settings with General, can change quantities. Preview separates those changes and shows before/after impact; Cancel changes nothing and Undo restores the prior settings within the documented session limits. Only insufficient product cover is raised to that product’s own review interval; other overrides stay unchanged. See [exact recipes and review/apply/undo rules](../PRESETS.md).
- **Pharmacy's automatic 99% model service target is removed after the independent synthetic gate failed.** Pharmacy now preserves the user's service target. The separate manually editable 99% setting remains available; it is not automatically selected by an industry guide. A model service target is not a guaranteed fill rate.
- **Shelf-life ceilings are opt-in.** Verified shelf life, age at arrival and minimum remaining life can constrain purchase estimates after MOQ/pack rounding, including safety stock; usable stock and MOQ conflicts are explained. A product's explicit no-shelf-life override (`null`, `0` or `"none"`) disables an inherited total-life cap without erasing actual lot-expiry facts. [Shelf-life rules and limitations](../SHELF-LIFE.md).
- **Lot/expiry review** includes FEFO commercial **Get rid of by** prompts, lot-total checks and input review for ambiguous or month-only dates. These are planning/clearance estimates, not food or medicine safety/compliance controls.
- **Display-only cost and profit information** uses supplied costs/prices and leaves unknown facts unknown; it is distinct from separately opting into a [profit-based purchase suggestion](../PROFIT-ORDER.md). See [cost/profit display formulas and limitations](../COSTS.md).
- **Settings reference reconciled:** the [guide](../GUIDE.md#settings) lists all supported supplier/product override fields, including profit opt-in, sale price, shelf-life facts and salvage percentage.

## Synthetic gate evidence

The original redesigned comparison, including the automatic Pharmacy 99% setting, completed **1,680/1,680 runs** and was **NOT_QUALIFIED**. At 30-day cadence, Pharmacy's aggregate net-profit delta versus General was approximately **−1,649**, with a 95% interval of **[−2,906, −401]** and approximately zero fill-rate gain. At 7-day cadence the aggregate was inconclusive, but one family showed significant profit harm, so the weekly Pharmacy gate also failed. [Archived gate summary](../history/changes/redesigned-gate-both.json).

Removing only the automatic 99% target produced **QUALIFIED_SYNTHETIC_ONLY**, again with **1,680/1,680 runs** complete. The five non-General guides matched General in the tested 7- and 30-day comparisons; General is the baseline. The archived quantity proof reports zero changed order-quantity sequences in all ten no-99 guide/cadence comparisons. This demonstrates a synthetic quantity no-op, **not superiority**, a service guarantee or real-world performance. [No-99 gate summary](../history/changes/redesigned-gate-no99.json) · [quantity proof for both comparisons](../history/changes/redesigned-gate-quantity-proof.json).

These JSON files are unchanged copies of existing independent synthetic reports, not a new gate run by this documentation lane. The summaries retain their original manifests and fingerprints; detailed per-run outputs remain in the original stress results.

## Import safety

Set-aside/rejected-row CSV downloads now share the normal order export's cell helper. Direct formula markers and markers after whitespace, LF or BOM are neutralized with an apostrophe; source headers, rejection reasons and source cells receive the same protection. Original imported cells remain unchanged in memory.

Import safety also preserves unknown blank lot quantities, gates ambiguous transaction dates and lot-total mismatches, honors numeric Excel expiry dates in 1904 workbooks without relying on cell style, preserves quoted CR in Python CSV roundtrips, isolates inventory-number inference from optional sale prices, and keeps usable workbook rows when another lot row is invalid. [Reviewed scope and local verification](../history/changes/v12-import-safety.md).

## Known limits

The **most profitable amount remains experimental and opt-in**, separate from the standard purchase quantity. When an available suggestion uses an effective review/order rhythm under 30 days, or the product has positive `on_order`, Desktop and standalone show this warning beside it; CLI JSON and affected CSV rows expose `profit_suggestion_warning`:

> Profit suggestion is less reliable with weekly ordering or deliveries already on the way — in tests it filled less demand. Use the standard amount if running out is costly.

This adds a warning only: forecast, order and profit-suggestion amounts are unchanged. Disabled/unavailable suggestions and monthly ordering without incoming stock add no reliability warning.

Supporting synthetic evidence: the tested profit-enabled implementation filled **75.5%** of demand in the weekly screen versus **87.4%** for v1.1. Source: `<local-scratch>/v12/profit-weekly/REPORT.json`, `results` (`current@7: fill=75.50%`; `v11@7: fill=87.43%`). That report tested `e17c693`, not the newer `c187f8e` bytes, and is not evidence of real-business outcomes or a rerun of this candidate.

## Limits

Forecasts and purchase suggestions remain estimates; inspect your own inputs and quantities before buying. Nothing sends an order, spends money or writes to an ERP. Synthetic evidence does not validate real sales outcomes, medicine/food safety or regulatory compliance. This documentation reconciliation does not verify generated HTML, physical Safari/iPhone hardware, installed Hermes Desktop integration, a live ERP or a hosted release.
