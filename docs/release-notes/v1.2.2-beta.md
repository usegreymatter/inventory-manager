# Inventory Manager v1.2.2-beta

This beta makes unusual purchase advice easier to review and clarifies import, language and startup expectations. It remains a free, offline reorder planner: nothing places an order, sends a message or writes to your ERP.

## Check before buying

- **Unusual sales spike:** with at least four months of history, a month above four times the median of nonzero historical sales prompts review. Check for a one-off sale, bulk order or import error. Zero months are excluded from the median.
- **Possible dead stock:** a positive suggested order after at least three trailing zero-sales months prompts you to check whether the product is still sold.
- **Possible stockout-hidden demand:** no stock on hand (`on_hand ≤ 0`) together with zero sales in the latest month warns that lack of stock may have hidden demand.
- **Big orders:** the worklist and product detail show the review warning beside purchase advice. The default threshold is a final purchase strictly above six times the next forecast month's demand; it is configurable and is not an order cap.
- **Unknown incoming:** copied orders and CSV exports retain the caveat to check open purchase orders. Unknown incoming is counted as zero for estimates, not asserted to be zero; exported unknown quantities remain blank. Partly known incoming retains its known amount but still needs review.

These warnings are review heuristics. They do not automatically remove a sales month, change the forecast method, estimate lost sales or reduce a suggested purchase. Check the source history, stock, pack sizes, minimum quantities and existing orders before buying. See the [reference guide](../GUIDE.md#purchase-safety-and-data-freshness) for the rules.

## Small correctness fixes

- Identical fractional sales values no longer create a one-unit safety-stock buffer from floating-point residue. A constant 0.1-per-month history can now suggest one unit rather than two; orders for the existing whole-number examples are unchanged.
- Unset side-panel product settings no longer display “undefined”.

## Imports, language and speed

The UI is **English-only**. Arabic product names and recognised Arabic headers are supported by browser import; review the matched columns before accepting a file. This does not provide an Arabic interface translation.

Browser smart import, the CLI/MCP CSV loader and the Python spreadsheet-inspection helper share a safe name-based fallback when no product code column is found. Names must be unique after NFC normalization, Unicode casefolding and trimming; duplicates across warehouses also stop and require real product codes. An unrecognized candidate code column, including numeric references under Ref or Article No, must be renamed to `sku` or `Item Code`, or explicitly mapped in smart import. Generated codes carry a visible note and are not verified ERP or supplier codes; real codes remain the best choice for durable settings and supplier references. Renaming a product can change its generated code. The CLI/MCP CSV route still requires a product name, stock on hand and at least three monthly sales columns. Sharing the safe fallback does not establish full browser/Python spreadsheet header-mapper parity.

The browser can process 10,000 products in seconds. For the Python CLI or local AI/MCP route, allow 30–60 seconds on first start for a few thousand products; larger or more complex files can take longer. Timings depend on the device, input and settings, not a performance guarantee. CLI/MCP stock CSV limits remain 10,000 products and 2 MiB.

## Beta limits and availability

Forecasts are estimates tested with example data, not validated predictions for your business. Auto remains experimental and opt-in. Industry setup guides are not quantity optimizers or evidence of superior real-world results. Missing or invalid stock and sales still require data review; these warnings do not bypass existing holds.
